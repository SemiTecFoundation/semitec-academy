SemiTec Academy — how to start it on a Mac
==========================================

1. Drag "SemiTec Academy" onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. This build is not notarised by Apple, so macOS will refuse to start it and say
   "Apple could not verify..." or "...is damaged and can't be opened".
   The app is fine — only the "downloaded from the internet" mark stops it.

   Open Terminal (Launchpad, search for "Terminal") and run this one line:

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/SemiTec Academy.app"

   Copy it exactly, including the quotation marks, and press Return.
   Nothing is printed when it works.

3. Start SemiTec Academy from Applications or Launchpad. It opens normally from now on.
   Repeat step 2 each time you replace the app with a newer version.

Without Terminal
----------------
Double-click the app so macOS blocks it once, then open
System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to "SemiTec Academy was blocked..."
and click "Open Anyway", then confirm with your password.

On macOS 15 and newer, Control-clicking the app and choosing "Open" does not work any more —
Apple removed that shortcut.

Your courses and progress are stored in Documents/SemiTec Academy and are kept when you update.
