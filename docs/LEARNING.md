# Studying and downloading packages

Choose a `.stlp` from a source you trust. Academy checks its files, content, compatibility and study plan.
A package renamed to `.zip` (for example to pass an e-mail or learning-platform filter) can be added the same way
in Academy 2.2.1 and newer; an ordinary ZIP archive is refused.
If a file or field is missing, the error identifies it; ask the publisher for a corrected package.
Anyone can publish a package. The publisher name is supplied by the package and is not authenticated.

Use **By type**, **Course order** or **Study plan** on the package page. Order and plan are included by
the publisher. The study plan starts on your selected enrollment date. Projects have their own tracker.

Each item has **Download folder** in By type and Course order and at the top of its viewer. Choose a
destination; Academy creates a new folder and refuses to overwrite an existing folder. It includes the
whole lab/project/training, sources and supporting material. Open a training's `index.html` (or its
publisher-defined entry) in your browser; open a lab's manual or a project's brief from its folder.
**Download course files** copies the entire package content. Offline copies save their own local browser
progress where the training implements it; they do not synchronize back to Academy.

If a package offers **Unlock author previews**, normal learning and downloads still work. Enter the
publisher's password to include `index_preview.html` with all sections open. Without that password,
Academy leaves preview HTML out of downloads. This is also the default when the publisher has not configured a password: normal study remains open, but author previews are not included. Some publishers instead lock the whole package; in that
case enter the password before opening or downloading anything. Ask the publisher for the password.
After a preview-password unlock, a training's primary Academy action is **Open all sections**. That opens
the author preview and does not change learner progress; choose **Study normally** in that view to return
to the tracked learner training.
These are local access controls, not encrypted archives. Unlocking applies to your local account until
that package is installed again. Every successful package installation or re-installation asks for its
password again, even when your progress is retained. A wrong password changes nothing. After unlocking, download into a new destination to obtain
the previews; existing exported folders are not modified.

Your **account password** is separate: change it in Settings → My account using your current password.
The old password stops working immediately; installed packages and learning progress remain. Academy
does not let learners change a publisher's package password; request it from the publisher.

Certificates are optional at both package and individual training levels. The publisher/author decides
independently whether to offer either. A completed item/package without a certificate is still complete.
When offered, earned certificates appear in **Certificates**, using the publisher's HTML/CSS/JavaScript
design (older JSON designs also work). View, print or save them as PDF. A certificate is a publisher's
completion statement; it does not automatically mean accreditation or external verification.

Custom presentations may show **Learning steps** above the page. Tick each completed step to record
progress. Other interactive trainings report progress automatically through their integration. Opening
a presentation alone does not mark it complete.

PDFs support selecting/copying text and clicking internal links, bookmarks and website links. Website
links open in your browser. Image-only/scanned PDFs have no selectable text unless their author adds OCR.
